Privacy

We treat every call and message as confidential. Call recordings, summaries, and contact data are encrypted and stored until you decide to delete them.

  • Consent: every interaction includes opt-out language and the ability to decline recording.
  • Sharing: integrations connect only what you enable, and you can turn them off at any time.
  • Access: change permissions for team members to limit who sees summaries.
