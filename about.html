Our Mission

Empowering Businesses to Connect with Customers

We believe that every business, regardless of size, deserves a professional and reliable way to handle customer calls. Missed calls mean missed opportunities, lost revenue, and a poor customer experience. ViVpod was created to solve this problem.

In today's fast-paced world, customers expect immediate answers. Voicemail is a relic of the past. Hiring a full-time receptionist is expensive and often unnecessary for small to medium-sized businesses. That's where ViVpod comes in.

Our mission is to provide an intelligent, 24/7 AI voice agent that acts as the perfect front-desk for any business. We combine cutting-edge artificial intelligence with a deep understanding of business needs to create a service that is not only powerful but also simple to set up and affordable to use.

We are a team of engineers, designers, and business professionals based in New York City, passionate about helping businesses grow. We are committed to building a product that saves you time, makes you money, and lets you focus on what you do best.
